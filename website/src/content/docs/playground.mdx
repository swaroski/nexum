Configure who delegates what to whom, what the receiving organization trusts,
and its policy, then **Evaluate**. The page mints a real Ed25519-signed grant
chain and proof with throwaway keys and runs Nexum's verifier and policy engine,
compiled to WebAssembly, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

<Playground />

The decision pipeline here (`nexum-sim`) uses the same verifier, attenuation
and trust checks and built-in policy engine as the gateway, and a test holds it
to the gateway's results on every decision-API scenario of the
[official eval suite](/nexum/guides/evals/). Differences: approvals stop at
REQUIRE_APPROVAL, and nothing is audited.
